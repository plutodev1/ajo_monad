Host an Esusu circle

Everyone puts in the same amount each turn, and the whole pot goes to one member per turn, in the order they joined. The contract holds the money and pays out — no one has to trust a collector.

You’ll be the first member, and the first to collect. After this you get a link and QR code to share; members join with their own wallets and write their own names. You start the circle when everyone is in.
